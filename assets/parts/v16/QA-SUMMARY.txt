What changed
The renderer now deforms the mouth along its authored lip direction and closed-lip curve, rather than squashing a rectangular strip vertically. Intermediate sampling uses premultiplied color and alpha. Existing open/closed paintings are preserved; no artwork or new dialogue was generated.

Visual check
The encoded before/after comparison uses the exact same timestamps (6, 6.5, 7, 12, 12.5 and 13 seconds) and crop coordinates. The corrected lips retain the face/lip angle, and the isolated dark corner flecks visible in the old 12–13 second samples no longer appear in those samples. All eight expression overview frames were reviewed. The old dark flecks were reproduced before video encoding and were compressed lip corners, not transparent holes in the mouth base.

All 1260 head/body/voice parameter arrays are exactly equal to the frozen v15 timeline. Final mesh.bin is byte-identical (SHA256 5e2d1fb36d84925ecb31c8e6d036d61c3f63faaddf55ed42a0354db9b34b6255). The offline generator directly calls the frozen production deformMouthPixels helper with both original open and closed RGBA sprites. It does not reimplement the mouth geometry.

Scope and remaining limitations
This is an offline OpenGL re-render, not a recording of the browser UI or a browser E2E test. Eyes use authored open/closed sprites, not continuously morphed eyelids. The mouth remains a 2D two-endpoint deformation. Existing thigh cutout gaps and rough hair matte are unchanged and visible. No official Cubism export is claimed.
