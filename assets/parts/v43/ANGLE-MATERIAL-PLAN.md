v42 remains the active rendering baseline. No face-angle runtime changes or new motion videos are claimed here.

Inspection: face_fixed_v22 occupies model rect[235,1,865,683]. Eight expression-specific fixed images carry skin, jaw contour, fixed crown and ornament. Eye opening/closing and mouth opening/closing images are independent feature patches at authored rectangles. Nose and cheek shading are painted in the face base, not independent3D features. The face has explicit holes assigned to12 hair strands; hair_reveal_native fills hidden regions. Simply swapping a generated complete head would double hair and erase the established blink/mouth system. A synthetic depth cap already failed to preserve eye and jaw ratios and must not return.

This is a material acquisition gate, not approval of any unobserved output.

Required aligned layers per angle: skin/jaw/nose/cheek base with eyelid/mouth underpaint; matching eye shapes and open/closed lids for the8 existing expressions; compatible closed/open mouth contour and skin margins; forehead/temple/ear reveal; corresponding bang/side-hair boundary and root attachment coordinates. Preserve existing hair ornament side. Keep all assets in the1086x1448 model coordinate system and record landmark pairs: inner/outer eye corners, pupil centers, nose, mouth corners/center, chin, jaw ends, temples, neck attachment. Original frontal artwork remains exact at zero angle.

Interpolation must be between corresponded authored endpoints with a single opaque coverage, not transparent blending of overlapping eyes or lines. Independently registered eye/mouth contours must remain paired with their skin and jaw pose. Test yaw and pitch separately first; do not extrapolate compound poses without corresponding geometry/material validation. Near/far eye occlusion, nose silhouette and cheek contour must be explicitly authored rather than borrowed by deforming unrelated face pixels. Existing rigid v42 handling remains the fallback when required angle material is absent.
