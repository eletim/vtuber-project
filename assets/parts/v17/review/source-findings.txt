Diffuse mouth skin halo: independent source audit
================================================

Audited source snapshot: clean repository commit 78ede7b68d5729915f8916582973fb385bbfec05.
No production files changed. This directory preserves the before model, runtime
mouth deformation, all 32 diagnostic states and their fixed measurement masks.

Finding
-------
The broad gray oval is present in the static surprised CLOSED mouth endpoint.
It is already painted into the skin RGB of the generated v15 source, not created
by alpha holes, black fringe pixels, MP4 compression, or runtime interpolation.
All 3,932 opaque mouth-closed sprite RGB pixels exactly equal the corresponding
Lanczos-registered RGB in assets/parts/v15/sources/surprised_blink_speech.png.
The source has translucent-but-complete facial pixels; packaging deliberately
copies their RGB and replaces alpha with the broad mouth support's opaque alpha.
Source alpha in the measured skin ring ranges 214–240, median 232. The skin RGB
itself is dark; simply restoring source alpha would not correct the artwork and
would risk ghosting old marks. A proper fix needs consistent underlying skin,
with only authored lip/cavity/highlight artwork moving or replacing that skin.

At PSD coordinate (530,370):
  v15 source closed: RGBA [242,230,222,228]
  runtime closed:    RGBA [242,230,222,255]
  clean base:        RGBA [254,239,230,255]

scripts/build_expressions_v15.py builds each endpoint from the entire rest_crop
or generated RGB inside SUPPORT_POLYGONS['mouth']. Its narrower NEW_ART_POLYGONS
only gate eyes, not mouths. This admits independently generated skin tones into
a broad restoration sprite. The base underpaint is not the main outlier.

Decoded video frame 653 is t=21.7666667 s, surprised, mouth scale
0.078751456973431. mouthBlend gives 86.0935% closed endpoint and 13.9065% warped
open endpoint. The independently generated near_closed RGBA is byte-identical

Measurement
-----------
Each expression has a frozen pale-skin ring mask in PSD coordinates: fixed oval
center (554,360), radii (43,29), y=334..388, minus the union of all tested
lip/cavity/highlight footprints dilated by 5 pixels. RGB skin gates exclude
colored marks. Nose, chin outline, hair and strong cheek blush are excluded.
The same mask is used for all four states within an expression. 1,407–3,199
skin pixels remain, depending on mouth size. See skin-measurement-masks.png.

Proposed after-fix acceptance
-----------------------------
1. Re-use these frozen masks, not newly fitted masks that could exclude defects.
2. For surprised closed and exact frame653 scale, target |mean skin dY| <1 and
   <5% of measured skin >3Y darker than common clean base. Also inspect heatmap:
   averages alone can hide alternating bright/dark bands or a smaller ring.
3. Inspect unchanged lip shape/cavity/highlights and corners at all apertures.
4. Verify no new skin patch, skin pulsing, alpha seam or hole at threshold .14
   and at blending scales .06..14. Do not whiten away the intended skin gradient.
5. Compare all eight closed/small/open state crops and exact same MP4 frame653.
   The final encoded image must lose the broad gray oval while nose/chin/hair,
   body, timing, voice and approved facial artwork remain unaffected.

Caveat: base-relative metrics measure endpoint-induced changes. A gray artifact
already placed identically in every base and endpoint would evade this metric;
therefore also compare base/source crops to the before original art and review
the final face at normal user-visible size.
