# v42 face integrity correction
The v41 visual acceptance is withdrawn. A shared curved mesh and positive triangle area did not guarantee face identity. The quartic depth cap altered horizontal local scale unequally: at input yaw15/pitch10, sampled face edges ranged0.7443-1.2119 times their neutral length (v40:0.9128-1.0169; v39 and v42:1.0). This accounts for unequal eye width, central nose/mouth sliding relative to cheeks, and pitch-dependent face length. The parent independently reproduced it at2.5-4.3s and6.3-7.3/9-10s.

Only the facial yaw/pitch projection in body-rig.js is reverted to the accepted v39 portrait transform. Rotation in the image plane and small common X/Y translation remain; there is no synthetic depth cap or yaw/pitch cosine scaling. This is an explicit retreat from simulated3D face rotation in favor of the approved portrait proportions. True left/right/up/down views require angle-specific facial silhouette, far-eye/eyelid, nose/cheek occlusion and compatible mouth/expression artwork. No shoulder work, new image generation, PSD edits, or installation occurred.

All other production files are unchanged from v41: forearm shaft/wrist/hand materials, finger lines, acting sequence, body72px travel, hair acceleration/lag, UI and sync code. Hair follows the corrected head surface, so its precise trajectory can differ naturally. Original v36 PSD remains authoritative, no artwork change requiring PSD synchronization.
